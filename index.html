AboutHakkımda

I am a PhD student in the Department of Civil Engineering at İzmir Demokrasi University, where I also graduated first in my department and completed my master's with a 4.00 GPA. My thesis research and my role as a TÜBİTAK project director gave me intermediate proficiency in MATLAB and SAP2000, and my work on metaheuristic methods for structural optimization has been presented at an international conference. In my internships I built structural models in ideCAD and took part in quantity takeoff and cost estimation.
